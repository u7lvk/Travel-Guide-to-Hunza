Explore the Sights

A few places worth slowing down for. Hover or tap any card to see why they stand out.

Passu Cones

The Cathedral Ridge is a row of sharp, needlelike peaks that rise above Passu village. It is one of those views that looks even better at sunrise.

Attabad Lake

This lake was formed by a landslide, but now it is one of the valley's most striking sights. The water is bright turquoise, and in season there are boats and jet skis out on it.

Khunjerab Pass

At 4,693 metres, it is the highest paved border crossing in the world, and the gateway into China through Khunjerab National Park. It is also a good place to watch for ibex and snow leopards.

Baltit Fort

Baltit Fort sits above Karimabad and has stood here for centuries. It was restored in recent years, but the original character is still very much there.

Eagle's Nest

Eagle's Nest is the famous sunrise viewpoint above Duikar. On a clear day, you can see several 7,000 metre peaks at once, which is why people climb up here before dawn.

Altit Fort & Gardens

Altit Fort is one of the oldest structures in the valley, with a tower and garden setting that still feel rooted in the area’s history. It sits among terraced orchards and old stone paths.
