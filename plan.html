Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit to visit Hunza?

Domestic tourists need no permit. Foreign visitors should travel on a valid Pakistan visa; no additional NOC is required for Hunza itself, though Khunjerab day trips may involve a quick checkpoint registration.

How do I reach Hunza from Islamabad?

Two main options: a short flight to Gilgit followed by a 2-hour drive, or the longer Karakoram Highway road trip, which usually takes 20 to 24 hours depending on stops and road conditions.

Is Hunza safe for solo travelers?

Hunza is considered one of the safest regions in Pakistan, with very low crime and famously welcoming locals. Standard travel precautions still apply anywhere.

What should I pack?

Layers are the key. Even in summer, nights can get cold, and the sun is stronger at altitude. Bring comfortable shoes, sunscreen, and any medicine you usually rely on.
