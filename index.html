Hunza Valley

You come for the mountain views, the quiet roads, and the feeling that time slows down. Hunza has a way of staying with you long after you leave.

Key Highlights

Three things that keep people coming back to Hunza.

The Peaks

Rakaposhi, Ultar Sar, and the sharp ridge of Passu Cones rise above the valley like a wall of rock and snow. Some of the best views are simply the ones you see from the road.

The Lakes

Attabad Lake has a bright turquoise color that feels unreal, and Borith Lake reflects the sky like glass. The valley is full of water so clear and still that it almost seems painted.

The Culture

The valley is full of old forts, terraced orchards, and mountain villages where the Burusho people have lived for generations. The culture here is as much a part of the landscape as the peaks.

Why Visit Hunza?

Hunza sits high in the Karakoram, around 2,500 metres above sea level, on one of the most dramatic roads in the world. It is a place where snow peaks, old mountain villages, and wide open skies all come together. Whether you visit in spring, summer, or autumn, the valley feels different every season.
